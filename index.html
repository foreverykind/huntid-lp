FAQ

Questions worth answering.

How does HuntID identify tracks in a DJ mix?

It searches the set you're on against a catalogue of 900K+ tracklisted mixes and returns every track it can confirm, with timecodes. Audio recognition, for sets that have no tracklist anywhere, is launching soon.

Does it work on SoundCloud?

Yes, SoundCloud mixes and live sets, inside Chrome. No uploads and no tab switching. More platforms are next.

What do I get for free?

A free account gives you 10 searches with full tracklists and timecodes. No card.

What happens after 10 searches?

Pro is $4.99 a month, or $39.99 a year, for unlimited searches. Cancel any time.

Does it run on mobile?

HuntID is a Chrome extension, so it runs on desktop, which is where most digging happens anyway. On your phone you can create your free account now and install HuntID on your computer later.

Is every track guaranteed?

No, and HuntID won't pretend otherwise. Confirmed tracks are named; anything uncertain is flagged as unconfirmed rather than guessed.

Privacy Policy

SECTION 01

Who we are

HuntID ("we", "our", "us") is a Chrome Extension that helps music fans find tracklists for DJ mixes on SoundCloud.

Contact: foreverykindofthing@gmail.com

SECTION 02

What data we collect and why

Account data. When you sign in with Google, we receive and store your email address and your Google display name (optional). Purpose: to identify your account and provide personalized features (search history, favourites). Legal basis: contract performance.

Usage data. When you use HuntID, we store the SoundCloud URLs you searched for tracklists, the date and time of each search, whether a tracklist was found, the number of tracks in the tracklist, quality ratings you submit (1–3 stars), the number of searches used (for freemium limits), and the tracklists you save and tracks you mark as favourite. Purpose: to provide search history, saved tracklists, favourites, improve tracklist quality, and enforce freemium limits. You can view and delete this data any time at huntid.app/dashboard (opens from the HuntID extension — there is no separate login on the web). Legal basis: contract performance and legitimate interest.

Payment data. If you upgrade to Pro, payment is processed by Paddle (our Merchant of Record). We receive your subscription status (active/cancelled), subscription period end date, and Paddle subscription ID. We never receive or store your credit card details. Legal basis: contract performance.

Analytics data. We use Google Analytics 4 and PostHog to understand how visitors use huntid.app: pages visited, time spent, scroll depth, browser type, country (anonymized), and session recordings on huntid.app (inputs masked). These tools load only after you accept cookies via the consent banner. No analytics data is collected inside the Chrome Extension itself. Legal basis: consent.
